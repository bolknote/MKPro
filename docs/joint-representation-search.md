# Joint representations, regions and layout

## Joint conditional constraints

The complete-artifact search now has a separate constraint-carrying extension.
A representation proposal contains its relational data/stack certificate and
the physical addresses that its runtime selectors must eventually reach. It is
not an executable artifact and cannot be published by the ordinary pass API.

The search composes finite encodings, interference-checked register-web
equalities and effect-bisimilar region sharing while these addresses remain
unresolved. All labels retain command identity. Encodings may grow locally;
register recolorings may save nothing until a subsequent region merge.

Closure solves the conjunction, rather than demanding a placement after each
individual rewrite. Immutable selector destinations constrain one component
permutation. Stable address-only or certified projected preloads are variables:
their final words are synthesized from the chosen target positions using the
existing data-projection and selector-writeback proofs. Generated setup,
observable raw data, mutable selectors and conflicting aliases are not free
variables. Compiler-owned decimal charges are rebound only through their
existing certified binder.

The finalizer sees only a closed CFG with transported entries and return frames.
Stack/X1/X2 equivalence belongs to each semantic edge; an address solution
cannot excuse a missing semantic proof. Selector-based path pruning is disabled
inside a deferred representation proof because speculative physical addresses
are not runtime facts.

The existing fully finalized compiler winner remains independent. The extension
uses deterministic, separately bounded lanes for representation and allocation
choices (12 expansions, 64 identities, depth 6, at most 4 temporary cells).
Exhaustion means no result for unexplored combinations, never equivalence or
unsatisfiability. Only a completely finalized strict size improvement may replace
the incumbent. Reports include the composed rule chain and synthesized selector
words in the ordinary JSON/explain optimization list.


The optimizer keeps three kinds of proof-valid alternatives in one bounded
search. It does not inspect source names, game names, chosen register letters,
article constants or the spelling of helper labels.

## Effect-labelled multi-entry regions

Partition refinement compares instruction effects and ordered CFG successors.
A call includes its callee and continuation; conditional edges retain their
direction. Maximal physical fallthrough chains can share one implementation
when every executable position has the same equivalence class. All internal
entry labels remain aliases of their corresponding instruction. Call provenance
is unioned, not silently lost.

No jump, call or return is inserted. Consequently every paired execution
performs the same state-changing opcodes, including their stack, X1 and X2
effects. The authoritative execution-context relocation checker independently
checks the resulting entries, branches and return frames. Manual instructions
and stops are unique observations: their components cannot be folded, and
manual components retain their physical positions during placement. Independent
internal procedures may still be folded in a program with manual input.
Raw code, executable operands and unsupported formal counters reject the rewrite.
Unchanged indirect selectors retain their exact physical destinations.

Operand mismatches also generate allocator requests. The existing
reaching-definition web allocator proves interference, indirect aliases and
entry-live values before recoloring. Recoloring is only an enabling alternative;
it is not itself evidence that two regions can be shared.
The transaction search enables those allocator requests independently of the
ordinary copy-deletion option. It also revisits the cleaned symbolic root once,
because earlier dead stores can hide a common region or join distinct lifetimes.

## Joint data/address words

A finite mode is described by all of its consumer-output columns and opaque
logical destinations. The solver intersects decoder preimages with exact
stable-selector constraints. A word is independently checked against every
data role, decoded address and selector write-back. Equal logical destinations
must use equal addresses; different destinations cannot accidentally collapse.

The currently certified numeric domain is ordinary decimal keyboard words
with at most eight digits, stable R7..Re selectors, and identity, negation,
sign, integer and fraction decoders. This is not a claim about arbitrary raw
BCD arithmetic. In particular, raw BCD cannot be normalized through a host
floating-point number, and Rf is not introduced into the stock-machine profile.

Literal/decoder shortening proposes a different geometry. The pass feeds the
resulting target positions back into word synthesis, rather than patching a
data constant's address digits without proof. Up to three synthesis rounds per
definition web are attempted. Component placement can also meet the resulting
absolute-entry constraints without adding padding or changing fallthrough.
Other runtime selectors remain pinned unless their own data proof authorized
retargeting. The finite-mode product proof still checks every definition,
consumer, alias, matched return, automatic-entry state and X1/X2 continuation.

## Composition and acceptance

The pre-layout search composes word representations, semantic sharing,
reaching-definition recoloring and existing callee-hole extraction. It admits
proved equal-size and bounded larger intermediate nodes, deduplicating complete
artifacts including proof and preload metadata. A root is limited to six
expansions, 24 admitted candidates and depth three. Representation alternatives
may grow by at most two cells; local growth is never published as a result.

The same semantic transitions are supplied to the existing post-layout
transaction search. That search uses the ordinary full artifact finalizer and
static proof gate. The unchanged compiler candidate remains an independent
incumbent; final program size has priority over a local pass's savings.

Each solver row retains at most eight address choices and reports that bound
as an incomplete search, even when other work limits have not been reached.
All bounds are deterministic work/candidate limits, not wall-clock cancellation.
Failure or exhaustion means no proved candidate within the explored domain,
not impossibility. The solver and layout engine expose their work counts;
accepted transformations carry their rule names into JSON/explain reports.

## Range certificates through context-sensitive entry operations

An optimization barrier does not erase every independent stack projection.
The range interpreter preserves Y/Z/T across sign editing while keeping the
opcode a barrier for code motion. X receives a numeric negation certificate
only for a proved signed literal or immediately after a direct recall; exponent
editing, unknown entry modes and raw instructions do not borrow that theorem.
This does not invent an X1 or hidden-X2 equality.

The preserved ranges feed the existing observer-constrained representation
solver. A bounded positive reduction operand can end a finite coefficient
lifetime without reconstructing its old representation. Unknown or negative
operands still reject that rewrite. Discovery uses def/use, opcode and range
facts, not a function name, source formula or selected game.

Compiler/ROM fixtures cover 55 sign-entry contexts, all three lower stack
slots, exponent-edit rejection, two carrier registers and two scaling factors.
The combined range/representation baselines are 40 to 33 and 43 to 34 cells;
their called-helper forms are 43 to 36 and 46 to 37. These are synthetic
integration results, not claimed savings for a pending example.

## Predicate observations across carrier epochs

A shared sign/zero branch does not force a finite carrier epoch and its later
dynamic epoch to use the same numeric codec. The representation generator
checks the complete finite truth table of the actual conditional opcode and
leaves ordinary dynamic inputs unchanged. The unchanged recall/branch pair is
an observation boundary, not a decoder: the relational proof still treats X
as unequal in the encoded epoch and follows both successors until every
observable register, stack component, X1 and X2 is safe. Raw words, changed
branch outcomes, unproved side entries and a leaking numeric value reject.
The usual positive-domain absorption certificate is still required at a max
boundary; predicate equivalence does not weaken it. Numeric demands are solved
to a fixed point on the matched-return graph, not by a depth-limited recursive
walk. Bottom and unknown remain distinct, so a grounded loop invariant can
propagate through long helper chains. Expanding intervals widen to unknown
after eight changes; work-budget exhaustion exports no partial facts. Neither
raw words nor independent manual entries acquire ordinary-number bounds.

The constraint search may use selector-consistent contexts only when all its
address equations already match the current geometry. Unclosed placements
retain the conservative call/return graph until their independent finalizer
has discharged the equations.

Compiler/ROM fixtures combine finite coefficient products, a dynamic max
reduction and repeated predicate joins, checking both branch polarities and
all four sign/zero predicates. Negative fixtures expose the recalled value,
the stack or a resumable stop, and reject a sign-changing codec where needed.

## Shared numeric facts on labelled execution contexts

The selector-consistent product graph retains its original edge kinds and
formal target provenance after expansion. Numeric proofs can therefore use
the same validated input-boundary classifier as exact register-value flow:
ordinary/error resumes and typed PP/R/S phases inherit predecessor registers,
not setup assumptions. X and the arithmetic stack remain unknown at input.
Missing edges, protocol anchors or matching return contexts reject transport.

Positive integral FL counters are refined separately on each labelled edge:
one falls through unchanged; a larger value decrements and branches. Zero,
negative and fractional values are not ordinary decrements on the ROM and
do not receive this certificate. Merged branch targets join both alternatives.

The interval domain encloses ordinary decimal arithmetic with integer
floor/ceiling endpoints, including fractional addition, subtraction, products,
squares and division away from zero. Scientific literals use their actual
runtime mantissa/exponent, never a lowered data-role value. Unsupported raw
words, overflow and widening remain unknown. Indirect stores use weak updates
over their proved may-targets; selector write-back comes from the existing
machine-address evaluator rather than presumed register stability.

These facts feed the normal representation search. A compiler/ROM fixture
combines FL, fractional arithmetic, runtime constants and a mixed finite/dynamic
carrier; negative cases with unknown coefficients and input provenance reject.

## Exact entry values without an assumed stack lift

The numeric range proof consumes exact X facts from the shared entry-buffer
interpreter, indexed by authoritative execution state rather than physical
instruction. Integer entry after clear or duplicate can therefore provide a
known value even when automatic stack lift is not proved. Return contexts stay
separate, and a public item query joins their ranges only at export.

This fact applies to X only. It never implies a shift or a value in Y/Z/T.
Resumed input forgets X and poisons the entry buffer until an entry-ending
command; fractions, exponents, in-entry signs and oversized literals require
independent proofs. Raw machine words still need an ordinary-number enclosure.

The joint representation search uses these facts without a source recognizer:
entry interpretation, call-sensitive ranges and finite/dynamic carrier epochs
compose to shorten the regression fixture from 47 to 40 cells with no additional
preload. Unknown and zero score arms reject the rewrite. Compiler and ROM tests
cover the final CFG, all registers, stack, X1, decimal-entry state and returns.
This synthetic saving is not a claim of a smaller pending-game baseline.

## Quantized arithmetic and edge-local entry values

The numeric must-analysis keeps an optional decimal lattice alongside each
ordinary interval. A lattice denotes every value `n / d` between its integer
numerator bounds, not a recognized source formula. Transfers cover addition,
subtraction, multiplication, division by an exact constant, signed fraction,
truncation, absolute value and square. Joins retain a common lattice denominator
across control-flow and caller contexts.

A non-integral lattice is retained only if its denominator factors into powers
of two and five and the complete result set is exactly representable with eight
decimal digits. Nonterminating division and operations requiring rounding lose
this certificate; the existing outward ordinary interval remains available.
Thus division/fraction/scaling can recover integrality, but `1 / 3 * 3` is not
silently treated as an identity. Strict fractional endpoints come from the
proved lattice, not from treating a closed interval as an open one.

Exact X words are also exported after each instruction, before edge effects.
This preserves separate literal results until the range analysis joins their
incoming edges. Resumable stops still discard X: their displayed value is not
an input fact. The ordinary direct-recall/next-digit lift is a separate ROM fact,
checked for R0..Re across prior entry modes. It does not certify stack lift after
arbitrary flow, clear, duplicate or unlinked manual entry.

The production joint search composes these facts with finite/dynamic carrier
selection. Its dedicated compiler/ROM baseline shrinks from 61 to 54 cells
without new preloads. An unknown score arm, raw words and unproved rounding
reject the corresponding certificate. The tests retain complete register,
stack, last-X, decimal-entry and return observations. This synthetic baseline
does not change the pending-game size contract.


## Finite-resource loop invariants

A separate relational analysis proves progress for an OR-only state register.
Native OR returns a canonical word with seven four-bit payload digits. Those
28 bits cannot be cleared by another OR. After at most one initial
canonicalization, a nonzero comparison of the old and updated word consumes
at least one previously clear bit. This gives a conservative bound of 29
traversals of a proved growth edge, independent of source names, board sizes,
mask constants, register assignment or an assumed number of game turns.

The proof checks every reachable write, indirect alias, selector mutation and
matched call context. Copies of the old word may travel through the arithmetic
stack or registers. A repeated producer invalidates its earlier dynamic value.
The consumed comparison must not reach its guard again without a new update;
otherwise a saved nonzero result could incorrectly fund an infinite loop.
Inverted zero tests retain the proper edge direction. Even when both branch
outcomes share a target, only the nonzero edge spends the resource. Unknown or
conflicting preloads, raw instructions and unvalidated input boundaries reject
the certificate.

Numeric analysis can use that certificate in an analysis-only product of the
guard's strongly connected component and the remaining resource. Outside the
component, contexts merge normally. The product removes only the proved
impossible next growth edge, preserves return frames and input protocols, and
does not change any executable instruction. Unreachable counter copies are
discarded. Counter phases are solved in dependency order so late predecessor
facts are not mistaken for an expanding recurrence.

This refinement is demand-driven: it runs only when an ordinary range query
actually lost information through recurrence widening. Unsupported arithmetic
or unknown input does not trigger speculative loop enumeration. The existing
work budget covers both analyses; exhausted refinement retains the independent
baseline facts and exports no partial product facts. A progress bound alone
does not prove that arbitrary input or a repeatedly updated packed bank is an
ordinary decimal value.

Compiler/ROM contracts cover all nibble pairs in every payload position,
the full 29-update boundary, resumed input, matched calls and returns, aliases,
resets, stale comparisons, inverted/coincident edges and deterministic budgets.
Composed size baselines are 50 to 43 cells, and 53 to 46 with a callee: the range
proof enables the existing mixed-epoch representation solver and joint search,
rather than adding a program-specific rewrite. Final observations include all
data registers, the arithmetic stack, X1 and a decimal-entry probe for X2.

## Deferred whole-family selector allocation

Stable R7..Re carriers are allocated to complete typed flow groups, not to
individual instructions. A separate bounded matching lane enumerates
permutations of existing families, replacement by direct-flow groups, and
release of an entire family to direct commands. It preserves the existing
search lane and incumbent rather than spending their candidate budget.

An allocation is conditional IR, not an executable artifact. The original
encoded destination is carried per register: a target label's provisional PC
is not evidence of the word stored in its newly assigned register. Literal
preloads are unchanged during matching. Final closure either places the target
at that word's destination or uses the existing independently proved preload
rebinder. Generated setup, mutable/ambiguous carriers, incomplete families,
opaque operands and noncanonical formal entries fail closed.

The lane reuses natural-target selector-writeback and direct/indirect X2
proofs, exact command-identity traces, external-entry ledgers, indirect-memory
facts and matched return contexts. Same-family stable-register substitutions
preserve the opcode's stack, X2 and number-entry transfer; both the removed
and introduced selector writebacks must remain unobservable.

Each complete root and its finite-domain common-processor alternatives can
combine allocation with a certified fallthrough split and projected shared
continuation layout. At most 128 assignments and 24 proof-valid candidates
are considered per root, with a 20,000-node matching work cap. All bounds are
deterministic; there is no wall-clock cancellation. Only a closed net reduction
runs the expensive downstream compiler finalizer. The previous incumbent
survives failed proofs, unsatisfied geometry and exhausted allocation work.

The growth allowance is measured against the immutable original artifact,
including cells already borrowed by common-processor factoring. Allocation
and bridge admission share the ordinary transaction search's overflow-safe
budget predicate; a new lane or parent never resets that allowance.

The compiler/ROM fixture covers a whole-family release costing one operand
and three shortened calls saving three operands: 15 to 13 cells. It checks
all registers (both carriers also contribute to the result), stack/X1 and
exposed X2, renamed opaque targets, deterministic
enumeration, forged origins, aliasing writes, generated preloads, partial
families and an observable conditional-X2 counterexample.

## Projected geometry of an atomic rewrite

The symbolic component solver can use certified final cell widths while
permuting the unchanged neutral IR. This separates command identity and CFG
transport from the address coordinate of a pending rewrite. Widths alone are
never an executable artifact or a semantic certificate.

The shared-tail transaction supplies its own widths: erased caller continuations
occupy zero cells, the helper return includes the inserted common tail, and a
converted indirect divergent call includes its new operand. Immutable selectors,
the side-space body boundary, and flexible-selector codec bounds are solved
together in that projected coordinate system. No filler cells are invented.

Only after neutral command-identity relocation succeeds is the certified rewrite
applied. Publication requires exact agreement with every projected label and the
total size, actual side-entry/return geometry, final authoritative control flow,
and selector data/writeback proofs on the transformed consumers. The complete
downstream finalizer ranks this alternative separately from ordinary closure.

A selector's original encoded target is retained independently of provisional
positions. Missing or inconsistent provenance fails closed. Rebinding permission
uses representable positive probes as well as zero: fractional codecs that cannot
encode address zero must not be mistaken for immutable data words.

Compiler/ROM contracts cover displaced helper geometry, retunable fractional
selectors, full stack/X1/exposed-X2 observations and nested returns. Negative
cases reject stale rewrite certificates, fictitious label widths, incomplete
projections, and missing or forged selector origins.

## Deferred fallthrough splitting and callback domains

The complete constraint frontier may split a fallthrough chain immediately
before an existing stable-selector target. It inserts one indirect jump through
an unwritten R7..Re literal, retaining its original encoded target identity
until layout closes. This is an enabling alternative with a real one-cell
cost, not a standalone optimization or fictitious padding.

Two command-identity proofs compose the change: a direct bridge may be bypassed
without changing external entries or return frames; then that bridge may use
the existing selector. The delivered word must be an exact writeback fixed
point. Numeric entry must already be closed on every bridge-entry context; other
entries bypass the bridge and do not supply that certificate. The
jump must preserve the arithmetic stack and X2. Numeric equality alone is not a
word certificate: ROM turns an ordinary selector 7 into the denormalized word
00000007. New accesses require an already full-width integer or a separately
modelled no-shift scientific-word fixed point. Raw instructions, mutable
selectors, manual/return continuations and missing word origins are rejected.
There are at most two deterministic split alternatives per expanded node;
the ordinary search's growth and final-size gates still apply.

Runtime-owned decimal callback targets contribute their 00..99 domains to the
same component solver as fixed and retunable preloads, including projected
tail widths. The final binder remains an independent check; it is not used to
discover that the solver's first unconstrained placement is unencodable.

Compiler/ROM coverage checks both branch paths, numeric-entry and writeback
rejections, aliases, full stack/X1/X2 and register observations, and that an
unrepaid bridge can never become a published size regression.

## Guarded definition ranges

### Finite-domain common processor

The joint constraint search can factor a two-argument projection prologue into
its shared consumer. One caller executes the projection normally; another can
replay it only on a proved complete finite integral domain. Discovery uses
call/store/recall effects, never function names, a particular formula, constants
or a program-size signature.

The certificate exhaustively evaluates every admitted input using the existing
exact-decimal engine, then compares all fifteen registers, X/Y/Z/T, physical X1,
hidden X2 and the incoming state of every unknown arithmetic operation. Merely
returning the same X is insufficient. Raw commands, mutable selectors, unknown
domains, unsupported operations, external entries into the replaced slices,
unclosed return contexts and exhausted proof budgets reject the alternative.
The unrestricted caller receives a separate symbolic proof; its input is never
assumed to belong to the finite domain. A typed continuous-input anchor remains
at the caller instead of being copied into the helper.

This is a deferred transaction, not a greedy size optimization. Existing
selector bindings remain equations until the ordinary layout/preload proof
closes them. The shared-tail certificate now supports ordinary calls through
closed linear prologues and a divergent call directly into the common body.
Each entry retains its own command identity during the side-space rewrite.
The unchanged incumbent still wins unless the complete artifact is smaller.
Two unrelated projections in the compiler/ROM fixture exercise the combined
neutral ABI rewrite and tail sharing, including the 53-to-51-cell size baseline.

Numeric facts now consume the same guarded definition/use exclusions as the
register-web allocator. The predicate proof accepts direct and authoritative
indirect zero/sign branches, including inverted selection arms. Selector writes
still use the original register effects; branch encoding does not change the
predicate or admit a guessed target.

A demand slice enumerates every strong definition reaching a queried recall in
its matched return context. Initial values, possible aliases, unsupported words,
raw commands and incomplete input protocols cannot be silently omitted. Only
proved-infeasible definition/use edges are removed. Surviving direct stores
supply their incoming-X ranges to that read, not to every use of the register.

A context is discarded only when its complete reaching-definition set is
excluded. Other paths and externally admissible entries are retained. This is
essential for shared procedures: an infeasible invocation must not poison a
different, feasible invocation, but one dead definition is not permission to
delete a branch.

The predicate fixed point, backward slices and optional numeric rerun share the
numeric caller's work allowance. Exhaustion exports no partial refinement and
does not erase previously converged bounds. The default fragment/input policy
does not opt into this complete-program reasoning.

Compiler/ROM tests cover indirect and inverted branches, matched calls, input
resume, known wide intervals, live aliases and stale payloads. The complete
transaction frontier consumes the facts with coefficient representation
synthesis: generic fixtures with different carrier registers shrink from 58
to 51 cells, with registers, stack, last-X and decimal-entry X2 compared in ROM.


## Mutating pointer ranges

The numeric analysis transports positive ordinary integer intervals through
indirect memory access: R0-R3 decrement and R4-R6 increment according to the
shared machine-addressing model. It does not confuse the selected memory
value in X with the updated pointer. Matched return contexts retain successive
updates; validated input boundaries retain registers without inventing an X
value.

Word representation is a separate must fact. Exact preload and entry spellings
must have the model's expected integer write-back before their interval may be
used as a pointer. Merging values also intersects that certificate. Fractions,
negative or raw words, zero-decrement and upper-bound cases stay unsupported,
as do indirect control-flow mutations. Losing only the word certificate does
not spend the numeric recurrence-widening budget.

An indirect store can overwrite its own pointer register after the update.
A unique self-target uses the incoming X fact; a possible self-target joins
that fact with pointer write-back. Unknown aliases or unknown written values
cannot produce a stronger bound.

Compiler/ROM contracts cover both memory operations, all seven changing
registers, decimal/scientific words, bounds, self-aliases, interval joins,
resumed input and matched calls. The independent mixed-epoch representation
solver consumes the result through the existing complete-artifact frontier:
two different carrier assignments shrink from 41 to 34 cells, with all data
registers, stack, X1 and decimal-entry X2 observations preserved. Incomplete
preload environments do not get this enabling certificate.

## Atomic selector allocation and literal erasure

### Non-executable jump annotations

A transparent fallthrough stitch may retain unused labels attached to its
removed jump as aliases of the destination. A label by itself is not an
executable entry. The transaction still rejects all direct, indirect,
external and return-entry references to that jump, along with raw, formal,
manual, procedure-boundary and protected label metadata. Complete final
CFG, selector, stack/X1/X2 and return-context proofs remain mandatory.

The tail-provenance compiler/ROM suite compares annotated and unannotated
direct/indirect jumps at identical size baselines, checks alias transport
and deterministic output, and rejects observed or protected annotations.

The ordinary greedy geometry is unchanged. Annotation-aware stitching is
opt-in and enters only the complete-artifact frontier, where full finalization
compares it with the independent incumbent. A local saving must not replace
the established path and force a larger downstream layout. An incomplete
preload environment cannot enable this alternative.

The complete-artifact frontier also opens whole stable call families before
erasing a redundant literal. Their direct operands are temporary allocation
choices, not independently accepted size regressions. The same placement then
assigns immutable selectors, respects all surviving data/address constraints,
and validates the final literal-consuming entry. Families need not overlap the
requested address: a permutation of unrelated register homes can be the missing
enabler. No register letter, function name or numeric literal identifies a game.

The extension considers at most twelve deterministic transactions, up to three
families and eight temporary operands each, retaining at most four alternatives.
Unrepaid debt is limited to two cells. This frontier runs only after the
established search and its family-release extension have finished. The fully
finalized winner is an independent incumbent; new candidates do not share its
beam or alter its equal-size tie-breaks. Each alternative must pass the complete
finalizer and become strictly smaller than that winner. Released address-home
reallocation is opt-in for this transaction, not a change to ordinary layout
enumeration. Existing deferred address obligations are transported across
erasure, not discarded.

A continuous numeric resume can execute either form of the same stable call.
The exact protocol, phase, callee and returned continuation remain observations;
the authoritative execution-context proof checks them. This exception does not
apply to a prompt, a single-step command, raw code or a changed protocol.

Compiler/ROM tests cover address-home reuse after release, combined erasure and
family allocation, full stack/X1/X2 observations, typed numeric resume and
negative cases for observed or written registers and altered input boundaries.

## Deferred callback correlation and selector seeds

A selector literal denotes a command identity even while an atomic transaction
moves that command. Data proofs must not compare its old encoded address with
a provisional physical position, or independently explore impossible callback
combinations. The deferred selector product carries an explicit map of encoded
origins, separate from an executable CFG certificate. It follows proved fresh
decimal charges through matched calls and returns and retains labelled edges.

Every indirect target needs a symbolic binding. Aliases of one command must
agree; distinct commands must not share an origin. Missing or contradictory
bindings, formal/dark counters and exhausted proof budgets fail closed. An
unknown charge still explores every admitted callback. In particular, pruning
cannot turn a reachable call with contradictory facts into a fictitious exit.
This view is for relational data proofs only, never command relocation.

The joint search can now place a packed producer's selector-equivalent value
before a future mutating access under that deferred product. First-use
truncation, speculative-store liveness, every caller, stack and X2 convergence
remain mandatory. The result carries its encoded-origin obligations explicitly.
A locally neutral two-producer alternative may compose with common-processor
factoring, complete-family allocation and shared-tail placement.

The extension keeps the previous search and its equal-size incumbent. Its
independently bounded lane uses the existing transaction limits, and publication
requires a strictly smaller fully closed artifact. Final selector rebinding,
data projections, layout and exact return-context CFG checks are not skipped.

Compiler/ROM coverage includes a 37-to-36-cell selected-callback fixture,
permuted register homes, provisional callback relocation, raw fractional
producer tails, aliases, missing/colliding origins, unknown charges, early
observations and budget exhaustion. The production search is exercised as well
as the conditional API. The current 4x4 combination still has 134 cells:
the additional tail saving is offset by address-family allocation growth, so this
extension does not claim a new size reduction for that example.


## Conditional regions in the joint placement search

An immutable fallthrough chain is not the only valid physical representation
of a branch. A direct F condition can be complemented while exchanging its
fallthrough suffix with a separately closed target region. The instruction
count stays unchanged, but region lengths and internal target offsets change.
This can satisfy address equations that no permutation of the original whole
chains can solve. The candidate still owes every selector/preload equation.

The authoritative relocation proof now supports an explicit direct-condition
inversion certificate. It admits only the complementary F opcode pairs,
exchanges the labelled target/fallthrough edges, and checks every rooted
execution context, address operand, external entry and return frame. FL
counters and indirect conditions are not equivalent replacements. Raw code,
formal side entries and moving manual protocols remain ineligible.

Polarity is not merely a CFG decision on the MK-61. A ROM counterexample to
the former local tail-branch inversion uses a dot after arithmetic: the
unprotected rewrite displays -1 instead of 1. The shared guard requires that
hidden X2 cannot be observed on either continuation, or that singleton value
and representation facts already prove X2 equals X. Direct F conditions close
numeric entry on both outcomes: a 168-case ROM fact covers recalled, open,
stored, Enter-suppressed, exponent and arithmetic inputs for all polarities.
A fresh digit may therefore kill the difference; a dot still observes it.
It uses the unrefined execution graph while selector positions are deferred;
a provisional address must not prune a real callback. The existing local
tail inversion now uses the same guard and full edge/return transport rather
than relying on syntactic branch shape alone. Safe local inversions still
remove their two-cell jump.

The new alternatives enter an independent bounded lane after the established
incumbent. The historical prefix of four neutral roots participates in
complete-family allocation, projected tail sharing and final selector closure;
remaining allocation slots admit the fair products described below. A separate composition lane
has at most four expansions, twenty-four candidates and depth three, further
limited by the caller's budgets. No game names, register choices or literal
values select this path. Only a strictly smaller fully finalized artifact can
replace the existing result.

Compiler/ROM contracts cover all four direct conditions, both outcomes,
multiple callers, deterministic address-constrained placement, the production
joint lane, raw/FL/budget rejection, every register, stack, X1 and dot-observed
X2. The unsafe local rewrite has an actual ROM wrong-result regression.


## Fair products of independently proved representations

A per-rule expansion lane is not sufficient when several earlier choices must
meet the same downstream allocator. Truncating topology alternatives in parent
order can fill the four historical slots before any selector-projection parent
is reached. Those parents can remain individually neutral and still be the only
ones whose polarity, allocation and pending tail equations close profitably.

The generic product-frontier collector preserves the established prefix and
fills only unused allocation-root slots. It expands bounded parent identities,
admits one proved alternative from each parent per round, and deduplicates
against both the established frontier and complete typed artifact identities.
Unknown equivalence, cycles and root-relative growth violations do not consume
an admitted slot. A prolific parent cannot spend every slot before the next
parent participates. Candidate order is deterministic, including equal sizes;
the incumbent never becomes an exploration slot or a new growth origin.

The joint solver uses this collector for the unvisited products of common
processor factoring, selector-seed projection and conditional-region exchange.
The existing allocation-root, assignment and growth budgets are unchanged.

### Composing allocation with validated neutral finalization

Complete-family allocation is also a conditional edge of an independent
composition frontier. It can follow a finite representation, and its closed
geometry can precede another representation, web coalescing or dead-stack
erasure. Previously these families mostly reached separate closure lanes.
The extension retains the historical incumbent, discovery order and budgets.
It has six expansions, 24 identities and depth four, intersected with the
caller's limits. Exhaustion remains an incomplete search, never a proof.

The optional intermediate finalizer validates and normalizes a complete
artifact without requiring an immediate size improvement. It must still
discharge the ordinary final CFG/static obligations. The compiler does not
publish or update its winner through this callback. Every smaller composed
result independently passes the publication finalizer, including a final
CFG check. A publication veto cannot be bypassed by a successful intermediate
callback. The original five-argument API remains available.

Allocation pays growth against the immutable root. Earlier savings become
available space; earlier growth reduces it. The remaining allowance is computed
without signed overflow and saturates only beyond representable cell counts.
No parent or locally chosen incumbent silently restarts the debt accounting.

Encoded selector origins stay attached to their literal words, not provisional
command positions. A later proved representation that changes a word must
decode it under the actual memory model and require the corresponding new
target equation. Conflicting carriers, missing identities, ambiguous preload
owners, generated delivery and stock Rf fail closed. This transport is
bookkeeping for an existing semantic certificate, not a new data proof.

A runtime charge is not automatically a private numeric value. The first
observer guard conservatively pins its original decimal targets when its
carrier is directly read as data, participates in indirect memory addressing,
or can be read through an unknown/overlapping memory alias. This also protects
physical last-X when arithmetic's ordinary result later dies. A more precise
epoch-specific certificate may relax this barrier in future; no such fact is
assumed here.

Compiler/ROM fixtures use different physical carriers and unbounded ordinary
or raw inputs, repeat the calls, and compare data registers, all stack slots,
physical X1 and decimal-entry X2. Only enumerated internal address carriers are
excluded from numeric register equality. A synthetic composition improves the
previous 46-cell winner to 41 cells (57 cells before optimization).
Negative tests expose an address through arithmetic/last-X, expose an ordinary
selector word, veto publication after successful private exploration, and
exhaust depth. Explain/JSON records the composed rule chain. These synthetic
savings are not a claim that the 4x4 program fits 105 cells.

## Selector-consistent epoch allocation and interior tail absorption

The conditional register-web allocator uses the same exact selector/return-context
graph as late copy coalescing. Operand equalities join demanded definition epochs,
not every value ever held in a physical register. Entry values, indirect aliases,
shared static operands, input phases and terminal physical-bank observations
remain interference/anchor obligations. Delivery rechecks the CFG and input/flow
certificates.

A separate bounded request family aligns a complete short chain with an interior
suffix of a larger chain (24 components, 10,000 comparisons, six requests). The
ordinary whole-region request generation is unchanged. The deferred folder can
absorb that short chain without adding a jump, call or return. It preserves every
label, semantic call origin and exact return/branch context. Indirect facts
deduplicate equal command identities after aliasing. Encoded selector correspondence
is explicit, not inferred from a permutation. Final data-word rebinding remains
mandatory. Nondeferred folding retains its original whole-component traversal.

A runtime address literal is not private merely because its register has no data
recalls. A ROM regression exposed the retuned address in Z. Closure therefore also
requires the existing stack-entry equality relation, initialized after the private
address store with X/X2 unequal. Unsupported or observable words retain their
original binding.

This proof composes two existing ideas rather than weakening observers:

- Reconstruct encoded target origins from compiler-owned charge digits and literal
  preload words. The deferred selector-consistent graph is a conditional data view
  only; unresolved or ambiguous origins fall back to the complete conservative graph.
- Follow transparent jumps/calls/returns from each charge to its actual private store.
  Start equality only at that charge's exact successor states (PC plus return context
  and selected execution epoch), not every invocation sharing the store's PC.
- The new generic prove_post_layout_stack_state_equality API rejects empty,
  duplicate, out-of-range and selector-pruned-away roots. Its continuation proof
  stays universal. The original PC-wide API keeps its previous behavior.
- A ROM-certified store establishes closed numeric entry. All later uses and
  observations still need X/Y/Z/T, physical X1 and decimal X2 equality under the
  existing typed interaction contract. Separate final CFG/binder checks remain
  mandatory.

The privacy result is cached only within the immutable PreparedJointProofInput,
including its complete source, preloads and control policy. There is no global
cross-program proof memo.

The 38-cell independent regression needs epoch allocation, an interior alias
and selector closure together and becomes 33 cells. Negative cases cover overlapping
lifetimes, final-bank observations, inconsistent origins and live address residues.
A second case shares one physical address store between two callback epochs: the
first word is dead and retunable, while the second is deliberately observed and pinned.
The conservative PC-wide proof cannot justify that distinction; exact context roots can.
No game name, game constant or source recognizer participates in these decisions.

The arbitrary-word privacy guard is not the only admissible theorem. An existing
DeferredSelectorDataLiteralLayout can deliberately use one charge as an exact
data value. For its complete verified family, closure replaces arbitrary-word privacy with
the per-mode entry certificates; the unique data-producing target receives the
proved literal equation, never a free data value. Every marked producer must belong
to the same selector family; unrelated runtime owners retain their constraints.
The complete exact-value, alias, entry and stack/return certificate is checked
both after placement and again after the caller's finalizer. Tests corrupt its
delivered decimal digit, family identity and register ownership independently;
none may publish.
